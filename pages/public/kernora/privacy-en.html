Scope and contact

This notice covers the project overview, support and privacy pages at aozkul.github.io/kernora/. The contact for these pages is Ali Orkun Özkul: ali.ozkul@icloud.com.

How the showcase works

These pages have no user accounts, document uploads, payments or contact forms. They do not connect to the CRM API or database and do not access client, student or operational records.

Separate CRM and portal deployments

A Kernora CRM and portal deployment may process account, record, application, document, payment-note and message data. Enabled services, access controls and retention periods depend on the operating organisation’s configuration and privacy notice. This showcase notice does not replace that deployment’s policy.

This website

This static site has no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or contact forms. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and technical request information to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Contact and your choices

Voluntary support emails are processed to answer your request; app records are not attached automatically. You can email requests to access, correct or delete personal information in support messages. Messages are kept as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.
